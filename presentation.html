The Problem

Fully autonomous vehicles are appearing on London streets under the Automated Vehicles Act 2024.


A member of the public, TfL officer, or inspector sees a vehicle parked — and has no single way to verify:

  • Is the vehicle registered and compliant?
  • Is there a licensed operator accountable?
  • Is it authorised to be here?

Who Uses This?

🏛 TfL Compliance Officer
Instant pass/fail with evidence trail — no manual register trawling.
🛡 Public Safety Inspector
Confirm a valid licensed operator is accountable for the vehicle.
🚦 Traffic Management
Verify the vehicle is in a TfL-designated AV operation zone.
👤 Member of the Public
Quick green/red legitimacy check — with a one-tap report action.

Context: AV Act 2024

The Automated Vehicles Act 2024 (c.13) is the primary UK legislation governing self-driving vehicles.


NUIC — No User In Control

Operators of fully autonomous vehicles where no human is in control. Licensed by the Secretary of State via CCAV — separate from (but alongside) a TfL Private Hire Operator licence.

Licence StatusMeaning
ActiveFully licensed, vehicles may operate
Under ReviewOperations restricted pending CCAV review
SuspendedOperations must cease immediately
RevokedLicence void — vehicles seized

Edge Cases

Alternative Input: Imagery & ANPR

  • Members of the public may not know or want to type a licence plate
  • Use phone camera imagery as input — snap a photo of the vehicle
  • ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition) extracts the plate automatically
  • Enables integration with existing CCTV / traffic camera infrastructure
  • Opens the door to bulk / automated compliance sweeps across zones

Camera input lowers the barrier for public reporting and scales enforcement beyond manual checks
